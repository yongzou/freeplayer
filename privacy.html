FreePlayer: Watch Audio

100% Free, Offline-First Audio Player for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.

Support & Contact

FreePlayer lets you import your own .m4a, .mp3, .m4b, .aac, .wav, and .flac audio files from the Files app, AirDrop, or local Wi-Fi and sync them to your Apple Watch for standalone offline listening.

For questions, feature requests, or bug reports, contact: zouyong909@gmail.com

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

1. Data Collection: FreePlayer does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data, analytics, or usage tracking information on external servers.

2. Local Storage Only: All imported audio files, collections, and playback positions are stored strictly locally on your iPhone, iPad, and paired Apple Watch.

3. Local Network & Watch Connectivity: When you use the optional "Import from Computer" Wi-Fi transfer feature, files are transferred directly between your computer and your iPhone over your local Wi-Fi network. When syncing to Apple Watch, files are transferred directly via Apple's WatchConnectivity framework.

4. Contact: If you have any privacy questions, email zouyong909@gmail.com.
